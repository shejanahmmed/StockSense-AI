Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2026 | StockSense AI Data Sovereignty & Security Commitment

Sections

Scope & Introduction Local-First Custody CSV Ingestion Policies Forecasting & ML LLM Context Processing Session Security Right to Erasure Inquiries & Compliance

At StockSense AI, we treat your business intelligence with enterprise-grade respect. We design our prediction algorithms, storage parameters, and deep-learning endpoints around an absolute dedication to data minimization and client-side control.

StockSense AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your corporate and operational data. This policy details how we treat uploaded multi-product CSV sales data, authentication credentials, and AI inference requests when you use the StockSense AI platform.

1. Local-First & Client-Side Sovereignty

StockSense AI prioritizes local data custody. Your active inventory parameters, transaction records, and historical margins are cached locally in your secure client environment. The primary system store operates on an isolated, containerized SQLite database. All credentials required to authorize access are locally compiled and secured using cryptographic SHA-256 hashing algorithms.

2. CSV Sales Dataset Ingestion

When you leverage the bulk upload tool to upload inventory or historic sales CSVs:

  • Processing Boundary: Data parsed from your CSV file (including timestamps, item quantities, prices, and event contexts) is isolated and stored within your private container instance.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: StockSense AI does not aggregate, compile, sell, or rent your transactional histories, product names, or pricing strategies to third parties.

3. Forecasting Algorithms & ML Engines

Demand forecasting, safety stock thresholds, and replenishment intervals are computed using mathematical models (such as the Prophet Forecasting Model) running within isolated python subprocesses. There is no external pooling of prediction weights across client instances, ensuring your competitive trading margins remain entirely proprietary.

4. Large Language Model (LLM) Processing

For strategic business insight synthesis and natural language chat interactions:

  • Contextual Queries: The platform formats raw metrics (e.g. holiday calendar correlation, promotion cycles) into highly filtered operational prompts.
  • Zero-Data Retention API: LLM requests are dispatched using secure HTTPS tunnels to partner API gateways governed under enterprise clauses: no prompts, historical payloads, or forecasting outputs are preserved by the provider or used to train public base models.

5. Token Authorization & Session Security

Access to backend routes (FastAPI) is governed by JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Session tokens are cached in temporary local storage (`localStorage`) and expire automatically after a predefined duration. This prevents session hijacking and maintains stateless API integrity.

6. Absolute Purge & Data Erasure Right

In alignment with data minimization principles (such as GDPR Art. 17 "Right to Erasure"), you maintain absolute control over the data lifecycle. You can execute a complete and irreversible purge of all local storage records, session indicators, trained ML parameters, and SQL data entries by using the "Danger Zone" option located in your System Settings tab.

7. Enterprise Inquiries & Compliance

If your legal department requires a customized Data Processing Addendum (DPA) or if you have specific regulatory compliance questions, please contact our privacy compliance desk at support@stocksense.ai.

For custom hosting arrangements or on-premises deployment support, please coordinate with your account executive.

Getting Started in 5 Steps

01

Create Your Account

On the login screen, click "Need an account? Sign Up" and enter your Organization Name, a secure password, and your Industry Vertical (e.g. Electronics, Apparel). This creates an isolated, SHA-256 hashed profile stored locally — no external server receives your credentials.

Already have an account? Just type your organization name and password on the login screen and click Access Dashboard.
02

Upload Your Sales CSV

On the Dashboard, click the "Upload CSV Data" button in the top-right corner. Select a CSV file containing your historical transactions. For fully optimized AI forecasts and replenishment models, your file should include the following 10 fields: date, product_id, product_name, category, sales_qty, unit_price, stock_on_hand, reorder_point, promo, and supplier_lead_days.

Under the Hood — How the AI Works

1. CSV Ingestion

Your sales CSV is parsed by the FastAPI backend. Dates, SKUs, and quantities are auto-detected, cleaned, and stored in a local SQLite database — no cloud upload required.

2. Prophet Forecasting

Facebook's Prophet ML model trains on your per-SKU time-series data, learning weekly seasonality, public holiday impacts, and long-term trend shifts to forecast the next 7–30 days of demand.

3. SHAP Explanations

Shapley Additive Explanations reveal why a forecast went up or down — ranking the contribution of holidays, day-of-week, and trend factors so you always understand the AI's reasoning.

4. LLaMA 3.3 Insights

A local LLaMA 3.3 language model receives a safe, anonymized snapshot of your inventory context. It writes human-readable strategic summaries and responds to your chat questions in real-time.
